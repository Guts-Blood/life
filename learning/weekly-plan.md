# Weekly Plan Draft

Created: 2026-06-13

This is a draft to update together. It assumes about 1 hour per day.

## Weekly Rhythm

| Day Type | Focus | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 days / week | Scaling Book | Chapter notes in `reading-log.md` |
| 1 day / week | Work-related agent paper | One mechanism summary and one disagreement |
| 1 day / week | Personal theory writing | One assumption note or one structured paragraph |
| 1 flexible day | Review / catch-up / rest | Update current pool and delete noise |

## First Two Weeks

### Week 1

Goal: establish the habit and connect Scaling Book to your current work.

- Read Scaling Book daily.
- For each chapter, write only the minimum useful notes.
- Do not add world-model material yet.
- Write one paragraph on "agent harness as training loop".

### Week 2

Goal: add one agent-theory anchor without breaking the Scaling Book rhythm.

- Continue Scaling Book.
- Pick one agent evaluation / harness paper from `recommendation-pool.md`.
- Compare it against your control-theoretic framing.
- Decide whether world-model reading should enter in Week 3.

## Review Questions

At the end of each week:

- What reading created real energy?
- What reading felt merely responsible?
- Which concept helps with a real work failure mode?
- Which note could become an assumption, experiment, or principle?
- What should be removed from the pool?

## Success Criteria

This plan is working if, after 4 weeks:

- You have 20+ short daily notes.
- You can explain 3-5 training/scaling concepts from memory.
- You have at least one written agent-harness theory note.
- You have deleted or parked more recommendations than you promoted.
- You feel more awake after learning, not more burdened.
