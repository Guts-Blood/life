# Verification Rules

## Good Assumptions

A good assumption is:

- specific enough to be wrong
- relevant to a real decision
- grounded in observed life, not abstract self-image

## Good Experiments

A good experiment is:

- cheap enough to actually run
- short enough to finish
- clear enough to interpret
- safe enough to reverse

## Evidence Strength

Stronger evidence usually has one or more of these qualities:

- repeated over time
- visible in behavior, not only intention
- compared against a baseline
- recorded close to when it happened
- relevant to an actual decision outcome

## Promotion To Principle

Promote a conclusion into a principle when:

- it has repeated support
- it changes future decisions
- its likely scope is understood
- it is more than a mood or one-off circumstance

## Re-check Triggers

Re-check a verified assumption or principle when:

- your life context changes materially
- the costs of being wrong increase
- recent evidence conflicts with the old conclusion
- the conclusion was only based on a short test window
